Try this

1
Themes: tap Classic, Dark or Modern in the popup, then open the Octane panel.
2
Backgrounds: pick Field, then tap or drag your finger across it.
3
Trades: open Trades for the WIN / LOSS banner, or New trade and tap items to change the deal.
4
Values: search an item in Item values, or build a deal in Trade calculator.
5
Friends: tap Refresh to see who's online or playing, with Join buttons.

Names, values and friends here are sample data. The installed extension reads your real Octane account and live values from Octomons. Source: github.com/wozers0/kerosene
